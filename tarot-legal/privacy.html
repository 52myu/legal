Reflect — how we handle your information.

This Privacy Policy describes how Reflect (“we”, “the App”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application and related services. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account information

  • Identifiers from sign-in providers (e.g. Apple, Google), such as a provider user ID and email if provided.
  • Optional display name or profile fields you choose to set.

1.2 Content you create

  • Questions, journey themes, hypotheses, session notes (“inner voice”), and feedback you submit.
  • Reading/session history associated with your account.

1.3 Usage & device information

  • App interactions (e.g. screens viewed, feature usage) to improve stability and product quality.
  • Approximate technical data such as app version, OS version, and device type.
  • Subscription status and entitlement signals needed to unlock paid features (processed via the store and our subscription partner).

1.4 What we do not collect for advertising

  • We do not use your data to sell third-party advertising.
  • We do not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID) for advertising purposes.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and personalize readings, journeys, and history.
  • Enforce free allowances and membership entitlements.
  • Operate, secure, debug, and improve the App (including analytics).
  • Respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.

3. Service Providers

We use trusted processors who only handle data on our instructions, which may include:

  • Cloud hosting & APIs — to run the backend and store account-linked content.
  • AI providers — to generate interpretations from prompts that may include your question and journey context (not sold for their advertising).
  • RevenueCat — subscription status and purchase receipt validation with Apple/Google.
  • PostHog (or similar product analytics) — aggregated product usage analytics to improve the App. Not used for third-party ad targeting.
  • Apple / Google — authentication, in-app purchases, and push notification infrastructure where enabled.

4. Retention

  • Account and history data are kept until you delete them or delete your account, unless a longer period is required by law.
  • Operational logs and analytics may be retained for a limited period for security and quality.

5. Your Rights

  • Access / correction — via in-app account and history features where available.
  • Deletion — delete readings or use in-app account deletion where available.
  • Notifications — control system permission for optional reminders.
  • Depending on your region (e.g. GDPR/CCPA), you may have additional rights; contact us via Support.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe we have done so in error.

7. International Transfers

Data may be processed in countries other than your own (including where our providers operate). We take reasonable measures consistent with applicable law.

8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards (e.g. encrypted transport, secure token storage on device). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy and will revise the “Last updated” date. Material changes may be highlighted in the App where appropriate.

Apple Paid Applications & Subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription or any paid feature through the Apple App Store, payment is charged to your Apple ID account. Such purchases are also subject to Apple’s terms, including:

  • Apple Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement (EULA)
  • Apple Media Services Terms and Conditions
  • App Store Terms of Sale

Subscriptions automatically renew unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period. You can manage and cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID Account Settings after purchase.

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled solely by Apple under Apple’s refund policies.
