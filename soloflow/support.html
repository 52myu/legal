2. Requirements

  • iPhone with a recent iOS version supported by the App Store listing.
  • Camera (optional) for receipt scanning; you can also pick from Photos.
  • On-device Vision OCR works best with a flat, well-lit receipt that fills the frame (or the document scanner when available).
  • Amounts and tax estimates assume USD.

3. Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Locally on your iPhone (SQLite and app sandbox). Invoices, clients, payment records, expenses, and receipt images are not uploaded to a SoloFlow cloud. If you delete the App, local data is removed — export a backup first.

How do I back up or restore?

In the App, open More → Backup:

  • Export backup ZIP — full restore package (not the same as a CPA tax export).
  • Restore from backup ZIP — you will see counts (clients, invoices, etc.) before confirming. Restore replaces all local business data on this iPhone.

What is a CPA tax package?

A period-filtered ZIP (CSV, optional receipts/PDFs) for sharing with an accountant. It is not a full device backup and not a tax filing submission. Tax numbers in the App are estimates only.

Does receipt OCR need the internet?

No. Recognition runs on-device with Apple Vision. An internet connection is only needed for subscriptions, optional analytics, and app updates.

How do I subscribe, restore, or cancel Pro?

  • Subscribe: open the in-app Pro paywall (More → Upgrade / paywall entry).
  • Restore after reinstalling: use Restore Purchases in More / paywall.
  • Manage or cancel: iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → SoloFlow.
  • Billing and refunds are handled by Apple under Apple’s policies (reportaproblem.apple.com).

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. See our Terms of Use and Apple’s Standard EULA.

Can I change currency?

No. SoloFlow locks the ledger to USD so totals and Tax Hub stay consistent. There is no multi-currency mode.

Are tax numbers official advice?

No. Tax Hub figures are educational estimates for reference only. Consult a CPA before filing.

OCR results are incomplete

Prefer Scan Receipt (document-style capture with auto crop when available). Use even lighting, hold steady, and keep the full receipt in frame. You can always edit merchant, amount, and date before saving.

Invoice due reminders

If you enable notifications, reminders are scheduled on this device (for example before a due date or when invoices are overdue). You can decline permission; the App will not spam the system prompt every launch.
