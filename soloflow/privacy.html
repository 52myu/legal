Privacy Policy

SoloFlow (“we”, “the App”), operated by SoloFlow Team
Last updated: September 19, 2026

SoloFlow is a local-first iOS app for 1099 invoicing, expense tracking, and tax-deduction estimates. This policy explains what data is processed, where it stays, and what limited network use may occur.

1. Summary

  • Invoices, expenses, receipt images, OCR text, and tax estimates are stored on your device (SQLite and app sandbox).
  • We do not operate a financial-data cloud and do not require an account to use core features.
  • Receipt OCR uses on-device Apple Vision. Images are not uploaded for OCR.
  • Optional subscriptions (Apple In-App Purchase), product analytics, and OTA updates may use the network without storing your financial documents on our servers.

2. Data stored on your device

The App may store the following in the App’s private storage on your iPhone:

  • Invoice and client records (amounts stored as integer cents)
  • Expense records, categories, and deduction ratios
  • Receipt images and related file paths in the app sandbox
  • OCR text derived on-device
  • Payment profile notes / QR image you choose to add
  • App preferences and subscription status cached from Apple / RevenueCat

Deleting the App removes this local data. Export or back up anything you need before uninstalling.

3. Camera and photo library

Camera and photo library access are used only when you scan or import a receipt. Media is copied into the App’s private storage for on-device OCR and your records. We do not upload your photos or receipt images to our servers for storage or recognition.

4. On-device OCR

Text recognition for receipts runs with Apple’s Vision framework on your device (languages configured for English / en-US as applicable). OCR does not require an internet connection and does not send the image to us.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Paid Pro access is sold through Apple In-App Purchase and may be validated with RevenueCat acting as a receipt / entitlement intermediary.

  • Apple processes payment; we do not receive your full payment card number.
  • We may receive subscription status (e.g. active Pro entitlement), product identifiers, and anonymous app-user identifiers needed to unlock features.
  • Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions, or via links in the App.
  • Billing, renewal, and refunds are governed by Apple. See also our Terms of Use and Apple’s Standard EULA.

6. Analytics (optional / product-level)

If analytics is enabled, we may collect anonymous product events (for example app open, feature used, purchase flow outcome) via a provider such as PostHog.

  • We do not send invoice line items, receipt images, OCR full text, merchant names, or dollar amounts in analytics properties.
  • Events are sanitized to block file paths and free-text that could contain financial content.
  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT) is respected where required; we do not use analytics to track you across other companies’ apps for advertising.

7. Updates and diagnostics

The App may check for over-the-air (OTA) updates or use Apple / Expo services to deliver binary or JS updates. Crash or diagnostic data, if collected, is used to improve stability and is not used to reconstruct your financial records.

8. Data we do not sell

We do not sell your personal information or financial documents. We do not use your invoice or expense content for advertising.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the App if you are under 13.

10. International users

Core financial data stays on your device. Limited network services (subscriptions, analytics, updates) may be processed in the United States or other regions where our providers operate.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Material changes may also be noted in an App update or on this page.
