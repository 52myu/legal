SpaceCleaner

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Summary. SpaceCleaner is designed to process photos and contacts on your device. We do not sell your personal data. Subscription and analytics may involve limited third-party processing as described below.

1. Who we are

SpaceCleaner (“App”, “we”, “us”) is a mobile application that helps you find similar photos, duplicate contacts, and reclaim storage. Contact: support@spacecleaner.app

2. Information we process

On-device media & contacts

  • Photo library metadata and thumbnails for similarity detection
  • Contact names, phone numbers, and emails for duplicate detection
  • These stay on your device unless you explicitly export or share them

Account & purchase data

  • App Store / Google Play purchase receipts processed via RevenueCat
  • Subscription status and entitlement flags

Diagnostics & analytics (optional / aggregated)

  • App events (e.g. scan started, feature used) via PostHog or similar
  • Device type, OS version, app version, crash logs
  • We avoid collecting photo contents or full contact lists on our servers

3. How we use information

  • Provide cleaning features you request
  • Enforce free-tier limits and Pro entitlements
  • Improve reliability and understand feature usage
  • Respond to support requests

4. Data sharing

We may share limited data with:

  • Apple / Google — in-app purchases (Apple Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy)
  • RevenueCat — subscription validation (RevenueCat Privacy Policy)
  • PostHog — aggregated product analytics (PostHog Privacy Policy)
  • Service providers under contractual confidentiality, or when required by law

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are used under the CCPA/CPRA.

5. Permissions

  • Photos — find similar images and large media
  • Contacts — find and merge duplicates
  • Notifications — optional cleanup reminders

You can revoke permissions in system Settings. Some features will stop working.

6. Retention

On-device scan caches and Trash items remain until you clear them or uninstall the App. Purchase records follow Apple/Google and RevenueCat retention policies. Analytics data is retained only as long as needed for product improvement.

7. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards where data is transmitted (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location (e.g. GDPR / CCPA), you may have rights to access, delete, or restrict certain processing. Contact us at support@spacecleaner.app. For App Store purchases, manage subscriptions in your Apple ID / Google account settings.

10. International users

If you use the App outside the country where our operators are based, your information may be processed in other countries with different data protection laws.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
