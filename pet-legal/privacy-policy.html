Vivarium — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Short version: Vivarium is an offline-first reptile & aquarium tracker. All of your data — pets, logs, and reminders — is stored only on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

Vivarium ("we", "us") is an independent app development team. This policy explains what information the Vivarium app handles and your rights regarding it.

2. Data we store (locally, on your device only)

  • Pet profiles — name, species, photos, and care intervals you create.
  • Care logs — feeding, shedding, weight, water parameters, and maintenance records.
  • Reminders — locally scheduled notifications for feeding and water checks.

This data never leaves your device. There is no server-side account system, and we cannot access, recover, or delete your data remotely. Deleting the app or using the in-app "Reset Database" action permanently erases it.

No backups, no recovery. Because your data exists only on your device, we do not retain or store any copy of it anywhere — if your device is lost, stolen, reset, or the app is deleted, your data cannot be recovered by us, as there is no cloud or server-side backup. To protect your data, use the in-app Export backup feature (Settings → Export backup) to save a copy of your records, and keep your device backed up (e.g., iCloud or iTunes). You are solely responsible for backing up your device.

3. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect names, emails, locations, payment details, or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs and Vivarium is free of ads.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.

4. Subscriptions (via Apple & RevenueCat)

Vivarium offers an optional auto-renewing "Pro" subscription. Purchases and subscription status are processed by Apple and our billing provider RevenueCat. To deliver entitlements, RevenueCat receives a random app-generated user identifier and Apple's subscription receipt. We do not receive or store your Apple ID, name, or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

5. Analytics (PostHog)

Vivarium uses PostHog to collect anonymous product-interaction events — for example, which screens you view and which features you use — so we can improve the app. PostHog assigns a random, installation-specific ID that cannot be linked to your identity. We do not collect names, emails, Apple IDs, or advertising identifiers through analytics, and analytics events are not used for advertising or for tracking you across apps and websites. See PostHog's privacy policy: posthog.com/privacy.

6. Photos & camera

Cover photos you pick stay on your device. The camera and photo library are accessed only when you choose to add a photo, and images are never uploaded.

7. Children's privacy

Vivarium is not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no personal information from anyone.

8. Your rights

Since all data resides on your device, you have full control: export it via your device backups, or erase it by deleting the app or its data at any time.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date for any material changes.
